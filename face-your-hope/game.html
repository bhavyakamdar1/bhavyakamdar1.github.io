Welcome

For years you built this ship. Now it's sinking, beginning to tilt toward rolling over. You have 5 rounds to save the ship.


You save a sinking ship by letting the right water in — opening the one compartment that counterbalances the ship and settles it level again. Each round you face a set of sealed compartments, and one key opens the correct one. You get 6 attempts to find it. Find the right key and you steady the ship — worth +2. A wrong key simply doesn't open; it only costs you the attempt, never the ship.


Can't find the key in time? Take the lifeboat and save yourself instead — claim number bundles across 1–100 and let the sea draw. Land in a claimed bundle and you survive, worth +1. The earlier you bail, the more bundles you get.


Increase Key Chance: once per game, in a single round, discard one wrong key — tightening your odds on the compartments that remain.


Double Lifeboat: once per game, double your lifeboat bundle count for a single round.


Reach 8 points across 5 rounds and you keep the ship afloat — you face your hope.
