11 Weeks for Better You

by Bhavya Kamdar

★★★★★ 5.0 (1 rating)

This book is not just about reading, it is more than that. It is meant to be your companion through the next 11 weeks of your life. In these 11 weeks, this book will guide you through a journey where you will develop a habit of learning something new every day.

The content is organised into five areas: Personal Growth, Health, Relationships, Finance, and Marketing & Selling. The book asks you to read just one chapter a day — five chapters covering five areas across five weekdays, with an activity or story on the weekends. Follow it for 11 weeks, and there's a good chance you'll come out the other side with a habit of reading and learning something every day. That's the mission of this book.
