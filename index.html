Preface

Thank you for opening this book. Before you turn the page, I want to leave you with the question I keep asking myself.

If your life were a movie, and people had been watching it up to this very moment, what would the audience be screaming at the screen for you to do?

Sit with it for a second. You will probably find that you already know the answer. You have known it for a while.

That answer is your hope.

So, can you face your hope?

is to risk.

About

Hi, I'm Bhavya.

I'm a Chartered Accountant who believes every number has a story behind it. I spend my days with risk, returns and the fine print — and the rest of my time writing, speaking and teaching about the things a balance sheet can't hold.

My book, Face Your Hope, grew out of one idea: to hope is to risk — and the more you act, the more places luck has to find you. This site is where the writing, the talks and the game all live.
